# AUGH! Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-03

AUGH! is a local-first Android sign maker. This privacy policy describes the information
handled by the AUGH! app (`dev.pschmitt.augh`).

## Information we collect

AUGH! does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It has no user accounts,
advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting service.

The app does not request runtime permissions. Sign text, pages, recent messages, colors, fonts,
animation choices, and other settings are stored locally on the device using Android application
storage. They are not uploaded by AUGH!. Removing the app or clearing its application data
removes this locally stored information according to Android's normal behavior.

Other apps may explicitly send text, page content, or presentation settings to AUGH! through its
documented Android intents. AUGH! processes those values locally and does not transmit them.

## External links

The About page can open the AUGH! GitHub repository, GitHub Sponsors, and this privacy policy in
an external browser. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies;
AUGH! does not control or receive that information.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes. The current version is always available
in the AUGH! repository. Questions or privacy concerns can be reported through the
[GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/pschmitt/augh/issues).
