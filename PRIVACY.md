# aughhhh Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-03

aughhhh is a local-first Android sign maker. This privacy policy describes the information
handled by the aughhhh app (`dev.pschmitt.aughhhh`).

## Information we collect

aughhhh does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It has no user accounts,
advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting service.

The app does not request runtime permissions. Sign text, pages, recent messages, colors, fonts,
animation choices, and other settings are stored locally on the device using Android application
storage. They are not uploaded by aughhhh. Removing the app or clearing its application data
removes this locally stored information according to Android's normal behavior.

Other apps may explicitly send text, page content, or presentation settings to aughhhh through its
documented Android intents. aughhhh processes those values locally and does not transmit them.

## External links

The About page can open the aughhhh GitHub repository, GitHub Sponsors, and this privacy policy in
an external browser. The pronunciation link in the README is also external. Those services may
collect information under their own privacy policies; aughhhh does not control or receive that
information.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes. The current version is always available
in the aughhhh repository. Questions or privacy concerns can be reported through the
[GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/pschmitt/aughhhh/issues).
