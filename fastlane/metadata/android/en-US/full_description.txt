AUGH! is a colorful Android sign maker for custom full-screen text. Build a deck of pages, choose an absurd presentation style, and swipe through it when the moment arrives.

Features:
- Edit and Present modes with immersive landscape presentation
- Multiple pages with add, delete, select, and reorder controls
- Auto-fit typography for portrait or landscape
- Modern, display, editorial, and mono font choices
- Rich text/background palettes, presets, and recent messages
- Still, scrolling, blinking, and flashing motion, including an opt-in full-strobe mode
